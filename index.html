Our Mission 🚀

Fresh favourites, on repeat.

We love to cook what we know. Planning sucks. Lazy Sous keeps dinner fresh and friction-free. Tell us when or how you want to eat, and we'll do the rest.

There are heaps of smart planners out there - and we love that. But we're here for simple. Like flipping Nana's cookbook to a random page and cooking what you land on. Not feeling borscht? Turn the page. Spin again.

Privacy 🥸

Your recipes stay on your device.

Your recipes, plans, and lists live in your browser. We can’t see them unless you sign in. Clear your browser data to delete everything.

What we collect and why.

If you sign in, we store your account details, recipes, and meal plans so they can sync across devices. If you join a household, your plans and shopping lists will be shared with other household members in real time. We also track anonymous feature usage to help improve Lazy Sous.

Who sees your data.

Only your household members (if you’ve joined one) can see shared lists and plans. Our service providers help us run the app — but don’t use your data for anything else.

How long we keep it.

Synced data is stored until you delete your account or ask us to remove it. Anonymous usage analytics are kept for up to 90 days.

Your rights.

At any time, you can access, correct, delete, or export your data — or contact us for help. .

Cookies & tracking.

We use cookies to manage login sessions, store preferences, and capture anonymous usage stats.

Security.

Data transfers are encrypted. Login is protected. Local storage is sandboxed in your browser. No payment info is stored.

International transfers.

Our servers may be hosted in the US. We rely on Google’s infrastructure and data protection standards.

Contact.

Questions, feedback or deletion requests? Email us anytime at chef@lazysous.app.

Effective: 25 September 2025
